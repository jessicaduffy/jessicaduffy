About

A little about me

I’ve always loved making things, from crocheting and sewing to ceramics, jewelry, and most recently, stamps carved out of erasers. I’m especially interested in how the process of making can impact an individual’s view of themselves and how it can bring people together.

I grew up in Guadalajara, México, and moved to the U.S. at fifteen. Later on, I studied Business Administration with a concentration in Marketing (and a minor in Art and Design) at North Carolina State University, and I recently completed a Master’s degree in Technology and Innovation in Fashion Design in Madrid. My background has allowed me to bridge cultures and approach fashion from both creative and practical perspectives.

During my Master’s, I became particularly interested in materials, sourcing, and production, while continuing to explore my own creative practice through making. I’m curious, hands-on, and always looking for something new to learn.

Project 02 · Garment

This top examines the relationship between the private and public self. Photochromic pigment reveals a lace brassiere when exposed to sunlight, reflecting how we modify and adapt parts of ourselves when perceived by others. The garment is constructed from sheets of alginate-based bioplastic, layered with piped detailing made from the same bioplastic with added photochromic pigment to create the lace brassiere.

  • Alginate bioplastic
  • Photochromic pigment

Project 03 · Collection

Inspired by bark, moss, and earth, this collection explores the tension between the organic and the synthetic. Layered, irregular textures and imperfections evoke the feeling of natural landscapes, while geometric silhouettes draw from the structured forms and early ideas of technology of the 1960s. The contrast between soft, organic surfaces and rigid, constructed shapes creates a dialogue between nature and the industrial.

  • Moss
  • Earth
